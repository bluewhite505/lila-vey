The story

She moved into a new apartment to disappear.

Instead, at 2:13 a.m., Mara wakes to find Elias Thorne in her bedroom—cold, controlled, and already far too familiar with her life. He admits he has been watching her. Then he shows her something worse: a photograph taken from inside her room while she slept.

As anonymous threats tighten around her, Mara is pulled into a hidden network of surveillance, buried secrets, and people who keep deciding what is best for her. Elias is possessive, dangerous, and impossible to trust.

He is also the only person willing to put the truth in her hands—and let her choose what to do with it.
