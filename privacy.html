Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lila Vey (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you visit this website, request a free preview, purchase a digital product, or contact us.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • Email address when you request the 2:13 A.M. Preview, join reader updates, make a purchase, or contact us.

  • Technical information such as IP address, browser information, request logs, and security data that may be processed automatically by our hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Communications you send to us, including support requests and replies to emails.

2. How we use your information

We use information to provide the free preview you request, fulfill purchases, provide customer support, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain the security and operation of the site, and—where permitted—to send Lila Vey reader updates, release news, and relevant book offers. Marketing emails include an unsubscribe option.

3. Free preview and reader emails

If you request the 2:13 A.M. Preview, your email address may be added to our reader list so we can deliver the preview and send occasional Lila Vey reader updates and book offers as disclosed beside the signup form. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in the email.

4. Payments and digital delivery

Payments are processed by Stripe. We receive information necessary to confirm payment and fulfill your order, but we do not receive or store your full payment card number. Digital delivery and access may be handled through our Cloudflare infrastructure.

5. Service providers

We may use service providers to operate the site and deliver the service, including:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Brevo — email delivery, contact management, and reader communications.
  • Cloudflare — hosting, security, backend processing, and digital delivery infrastructure.
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.

These providers process information under their own privacy terms and may process data in countries other than your own.

6. Legal bases and choices

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to perform a contract (for example, fulfilling a paid order), based on your consent where consent is requested, and where appropriate for legitimate interests such as site security, customer support, and preventing abuse, subject to applicable law.

7. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including order records, legal or accounting obligations, dispute resolution, and reader communications. If you unsubscribe from marketing, we may retain a limited suppression record so we do not send marketing to you again.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. These rights may be subject to legal exceptions.

The site may use strictly necessary browser storage or similar technologies for functionality and security. If we add analytics, advertising, or other non-essential tracking that requires consent, we will update this policy and provide any consent mechanism required by applicable law.

This site and its products are intended for adults and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.
